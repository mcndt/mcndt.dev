A fun, joyous [Donald Byrd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Byrd) record with catchy piano accompaniments, and drums that make me dance around in my chair. Exciting dialogues between trumpet and saxophone.

This album makes me think of a beautiful spring day, I walk out of the house and leisurely make my way to the bookstore or cofee shop, licking up the sunshine as I strut along. Perhaps part of that mental connection I have to thank to the “evergreen” modernist graphic design typical of [Blue Note](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Note_Records) releases.

A warm and crisp recording to boot! (or so sounds the 2015 remaster on Spotify to my ears, anyways).

Favorite tracks:
- *Ghana* (Track 1) has a great drums solo and is a very strong opening track.
- *Lex* (Track 4) is another great one. fun solos, expressive drums, catchy piano.

I think I discovered this album as a listening recommendation in my TikTok feed.
