This “modern folk” record sways between very tender, intimate recordings, and more intense rock sounds. Listening to Annahstasia’s singing is simultaneously relaxing and cathartic for me.

I have been listening to this album at least weekly for the past few months. I first discovered this artist via “professional music fan” Derrick Gee ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@derrickgee), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@gee_derrick), [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/gee_derrick?l=it)) who seemingly cannot stop promoting her. She was also a guest curator on his listening show! ([Solid Air 15 November, 2024](https://www.patreon.com/posts/audio-first-ever-115999203)).

Favorite tracks:
- *Waiting* (Track 6): This track perfectly highlights Annahstasia’s vocal range, from tender, quiet singing to intense, emotionally charged vocals.
- *Overflow* (Track 7): I enjoy the lyrics and meaning of this song, and appreciate it as a generally more optimistic, happy-sounding track. It helps me unwind and relax the most.
- *Silk and Velvet* (Track 9): My favorite track on the record. It has a great build-up as the vocals intensify over the course of the song. It also has captivating lyrics that explore the tension between anti-capitalist beliefs and materialist desires.
- *Satisfy Me* (Track 10): Another banger with beautiful emotional range.

Happy listening,

Max
